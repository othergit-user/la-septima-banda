## Objective

Review the supplied Stage 1 draft and strengthen it for production across Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, Instagram Feed, TikTok, WhatsApp Status, and promotional flyers. The update also establishes the correct boundary between creative production and the next-stage social media strategy.

## Audit Findings

The supplied draft had a strong strategic foundation, including the campaign promise, visual direction, 15-second Reel, three ad angles, funnel, audience hypotheses, and Canva checklist. The principal gaps were platform-specific creative behavior, TikTok-native treatment, WhatsApp Status sequencing, flyer hierarchy, audio fallback options, alternate hooks, safe-zone guidance, and a clearer master-template workflow.

The palette was also refined. Gold, black, and warm amber now lead the visual system in alignment with the supplied music-video reference. Sinaloa Green remains available as a restrained secondary accent rather than the dominant color field.

## Completed Improvements

The platform blueprint now defines a complete asset family rather than isolated assets. It includes a polished Hero Reel for Instagram and Facebook, a more conversational TikTok-native cut, a four-card WhatsApp Status sequence, three flyer concepts, digital and print specifications, CTA architecture, production checks, audio-rights fallbacks, and a master-template workflow.

The Reel was improved with a reference-informed detail-to-scale structure: brass or metallic detail, performer connection, synchronized ensemble movement, Phoenix group energy, and final crowd or arena scale. The TikTok version uses a faster conversational hook and a comment-driving prompt. WhatsApp Status is treated as a forwardable sequence with one message per card.
