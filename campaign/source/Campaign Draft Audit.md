## Executive Assessment

The supplied draft is a strong strategic foundation. It already contains the core promise, a usable visual system, a 15-second Reel outline, three ad angles, audience hypotheses, a lightweight funnel, and a Canva checklist. Its biggest opportunity is to become more production-specific and platform-aware: each channel needs its own versioning logic, safe-zone guidance, opening-hook system, audio rules, export requirements, and flyer hierarchy.

The draft also needs to reconcile its earlier green-led palette with the reference-video direction, which is more strongly characterized by gold-and-black styling, warm amber light, metallic performance details, and intimate ensemble energy. Green can remain a restrained secondary accent, but it should not compete with the gold-and-black visual signature.

## What Should Be Retained

The strongest strategic elements are the core idea, the promise, the official hashtag, the premium cinematic positioning, the avoidance of generic restaurant-flyer aesthetics, the three creative angles, the social-night framing, the group-planning CTA, the placeholder disclaimer, the funnel logic, and the audience segmentation.

The reference-video findings should remain central. The campaign benefits from brass close-ups, synchronized movement, direct-to-camera performance, warm practical lights, and a detail-to-scale edit structure. These cues are more ownable and culturally specific than generic crowd footage alone.

## What Should Be Improved

| Area | Issue in supplied draft | Recommended improvement |
|---|---|---|
| Color system | Sinaloa Verde is treated as a primary accent, but the reference video is more clearly gold, black, and warm amber. | Make Midnight/Arena Black and Brass/Mezcal Gold primary; use green as a restrained secondary accent. |
| Platform strategy | Reel, Story, and static formats are listed, but TikTok and WhatsApp Status are not given distinct creative rules. | Create channel-specific cuts, text density, CTA behavior, and audio guidance. |
| Reel structure | The storyboard is directionally good but begins with a generic black screen and lacks alternate hooks. | Produce three opening-hook variants and use the reference’s instrument-detail and performance language. |
| TikTok | No native TikTok version is defined. | Add a more immediate, less polished cut with creator-style text, beat-first editing, and comment-driving copy. |
| WhatsApp Status | Story is treated like a generic vertical placement. | Design a sequence of short status cards with one message per frame and a direct share/contact action. |
| Flyers | No flyer hierarchy or print specifications are defined. | Add poster/flyer formats, information hierarchy, bleed/safe margin, QR placeholder, and distance-readable type. |
| Copy | Some phrases imply confirmed event information or urgency. | Keep date and location marked as placeholders until verification; avoid “first row,” ticket scarcity, or implied exclusivity unless substantiated. |
| CTA system | CTAs vary but are not mapped to funnel stage. | Assign discovery, engagement, lead, and purchase CTAs by platform and campaign stage. |
| Audio | The draft suggests crowd and music cues but does not distinguish cleared music from soundalike or voiceover versions. | Create music-rights, instrumental, and voiceover fallback versions. |
| Production workflow | Canva is mentioned as a design system, but the draft does not define master-template relationships. | Build one master visual system and derive platform variants from it, rather than making isolated designs. |
| Social strategy | The draft ends before post sequencing, angle rotation, and hashtag architecture. | Reserve this for the next stage after assets and platform variants are approved. |

## Recommended Asset Family

The campaign should be produced as an asset family rather than as one Reel and three isolated flyers. The minimum first production set should include three short-form video hooks, one 15-second master Reel, one TikTok-native edit, one WhatsApp Status sequence, three 4:5 feed ads, one 1:1 square fallback, one vertical flyer, one print flyer, and one landing-page hero.

Every asset should share the same black-and-gold visual DNA, official hashtag treatment, verified event-information block, and conversion destination. The emotional angle can change by platform, but the identity should remain recognizable.

## Priority Corrections

First, update the visual system to make gold, black, and warm amber primary and green secondary. Second, create channel-specific creative specifications. Third, add alternate hooks and audio fallbacks. Fourth, define a flyer information hierarchy that remains readable at a distance. Fifth, separate concept language from verified ticket and event claims. Sixth, prepare the documents for a later social media strategy phase without prematurely locking posting frequency or hashtag sets.
