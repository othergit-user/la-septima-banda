# La Séptima Banda — Digital Marketing & Live Concert Campaign Master Brief

## 1. Campaign Overview

### Artist
**La Séptima Banda**

### Focus Track
**“Se Defiende”** — live-performance footage and crowd-singalong moments.

### Primary Objective

Drive measurable revenue and audience growth for La Séptima Banda live concert dates through:

- Digital presale ticket sales
- Physical box-office sales
- VIP/table reservations via WhatsApp
- Regional Mexican audience growth
- Retargetable video-viewer and engagement audiences
- First-party CRM and WhatsApp audience development
- Reusable creative and audience intelligence for future tour dates

### Core Campaign Concept

> **No vendas solamente el concierto. Vende la experiencia sinaloense.**

The campaign should make people **feel the energy of a La Séptima Banda live show before asking them to purchase.**

### Core Funnels

**General Admission**

`WATCH → FEEL → ENGAGE → CHECK DATE → BUY`

**VIP**

`WATCH → WANT VIP → WHATSAPP → QUALIFY → RESERVE → PAYMENT`

## TOF

Track:

- CPM
- Reach
- Frequency
- Video completion rate
- ThruPlay
- Cost per ThruPlay

Initial creative benchmark:

- 15-second video completion target: approximately 25–30%+

## MOF

Track:

- CTR
- Landing-page views
- Cost per landing-page view
- Add-to-cart
- Initiate checkout

Initial CTR benchmark:

- Approximately 1.2–1.8% as a testing reference

## BOF

Track:

- Purchases
- Cost per purchase
- ROAS
- Checkout conversion rate
- Revenue
- VIP qualified leads
- Cost per qualified WhatsApp lead
- WhatsApp-to-reservation rate

### ROAS Rule

Do not automatically assume a universal 3–5x ROAS target.

Calculate the actual break-even ROAS from event contribution margin.

Example:

If $100 in ticket revenue leaves $40 available to cover advertising and profit:

`Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ 0.40 = 2.5x`

VIP should be calculated separately because its economics may differ substantially from general admission.

# 23. Creative Testing Matrix

Test individual variables instead of producing large numbers of unrelated ads.

| Test | Version A | Version B |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | “Así suena en vivo” | “Esto te vas a perder” |
| Visual | Band performance | Crowd reaction |
| CTA | “Compra boletos” | “Aparta tu lugar” |
| Social proof | Crowd | UGC |
| Offer | Presale | Last tickets |
| Format | 15s Reel | 9s TikTok |

## Testing Rule

Determine **why** a creative wins:

- Hook
- Visual
- Offer
- Audience
- CTA
- Format
- Timing

Then scale the winning variable.

# 32. Strategic Operating Principles

## Principle 1 — Sell the Experience

The audience should imagine themselves inside the concert before they see the ticket CTA.

## Principle 2 — Make “Se Defiende” the Creative Engine

Use the track as the emotional bridge between:

`Music → Crowd → Experience → Event → Purchase`

## Principle 3 — Separate GA and VIP

General admission is primarily an ecommerce conversion funnel.

VIP is a sales/conversation funnel.

## Principle 4 — Retarget Intent

Do not keep spending heavily on cold traffic when large pools of high-intent users are available.

## Principle 5 — Use Real Scarcity

Only advertise:

- Remaining tickets
- Remaining tables
- Price increases
- Presale deadlines

when the information is verified.

## Principle 6 — Build First-Party Audiences

Every concert should make the next concert easier to sell.

Build:

- CRM
- Email audiences
- WhatsApp leads
- Purchaser audiences
- Video-viewer audiences
- Social engagement audiences
- VIP buyer audiences

## Principle 7 — Measure Revenue, Not Vanity Metrics

A cheap click is not automatically valuable.

A cheap WhatsApp conversation is not automatically valuable.

A high video-view rate is not automatically valuable.

The ultimate measurement is:

> **How efficiently did marketing generate profitable ticket and VIP revenue while building a reusable audience?**
