## Agent handoff: automated social posting process

### Mission

The agent prepares, schedules, monitors, and reports on approved La Séptima Banda Live organic and paid social content. It must never publish unapproved creative, change the event facts, or launch paid spend without explicit human approval.

### Inputs

The agent reads the approved event record, the ad-copy table in this file, the organic package in `social-media-package.md`, the live-site review in `live-site-review.md`, the persistent asset manifest in `social-assets-manifest.md`, and the current publishing calendar. The canonical event record is the table in this file; if any other file conflicts, stop and request review.

### Required content object

Each post should be represented as a structured record with `contentId`, `platform`, `placement`, `objective`, `language`, `copy`, `headline`, `description`, `cta`, `assetUrl`, `landingUrl`, `utmSource`, `utmMedium`, `utmCampaign`, `scheduledAt`, `approvalStatus`, and `connectionId`.

The `connectionId` must be generated once for the execution session and added to every transfer receipt, upload attempt, scheduling request, and reporting request. Never place names, email addresses, phone numbers, card data, or raw access tokens in logs.

### Safe execution sequence

1. Load the canonical event record and verify the date is August 22, 2026, not August 23.
2. Perform a same-origin health check against the ticket URL and confirm the page renders the AplusGlobal footer lockup and the security messaging.
3. Validate that every asset URL resolves and that every copy block contains the exact venue, date, price, and presale deadline.
4. Create draft content objects with `approvalStatus = DRAFT`.
5. Run a copy safety check for placeholders, unsupported claims, fake scarcity, invented guests, invented WhatsApp numbers, or incorrect prices.
6. Send a human approval summary showing platform, placement, copy, image, landing URL, schedule, and intended spend.
7. Do not post or spend until approval changes the record to `APPROVED`.
8. After approval, schedule the content through the chosen platform workflow and record the platform response ID, timestamp, status, and `connectionId`.
9. Verify the first live render manually. If the date, price, link, image, or CTA is wrong, pause the affected item immediately.
10. Produce a daily report showing scheduled, published, failed, paused, and rejected content, with non-PII connection IDs.

### Schedule rules

Use local Phoenix time. Publish the flagship Reel and first Story immediately after approval. Publish the carousel on the following day. Run the last-call copy only during the final day of the presale window. Do not create a minute-level polling loop. For recurring checks, use a low-frequency scheduled job or a server-side background job with backoff; do not launch a full AI run for every deterministic status check.

### Retry and failure rules

Retry transient upload or scheduling failures up to three times with exponential backoff. Never retry a rejected ad, a policy warning, an invalid destination URL, or a content mismatch without human review. If the live site health check fails, pause all new paid traffic and notify the owner.

### Security and privacy rules

Keep platform tokens in encrypted secret storage, never in Markdown, source code, ad copy, or logs. Allowlist the ticket domain and managed asset origin. Block redirects to any non-approved domain. Use connection IDs for auditability, but do not treat them as encryption keys. Do not accept or store full payment card data in the social automation workflow. Require human confirmation before any irreversible action, including publishing, spending, editing a live campaign, or changing the landing URL.

### Two viable operating options

| Approach | Tradeoffs | Cost | Setup complexity |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Platform-native scheduling with a human approval queue | Fastest and lowest risk for this deadline; less automation and reporting depth | Usually included with the platform account; ad spend separate | Low |
| Managed automation runner with platform APIs and a small approval dashboard | Enables structured content records, connection IDs, retries, UTM generation, and reporting; requires API access, secrets, and maintenance | Hosting and platform/API costs vary; ad spend separate | Medium to high |

For the immediate deadline, use the first approach for publication and treat the second as the durable follow-up implementation. The agent handoff above is designed so the process can move to the second approach without rewriting the copy library.
